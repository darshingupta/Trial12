# RC 800 Sectional 4

A 24-question reading-comprehension sectional (4 passages × 6 questions, 45 minutes, +1 / −0.25) in Countdown CLAT formats at GMAT 800 / CAT difficulty. Every passage carries at least one strengthen/weaken question and at least one assumption/conclusion question.

Passages (all original writing): philosophy (promises to oneself), microbiology (antibiotic persisters), modern Indian history (the colonial railways debate), literary criticism (editing revised texts).

- `RC_800_Sectional_4_Paper.docx`: question booklet with cover page and answer sheet. No answers inside.
- `RC_800_Sectional_4_Answer_Key.docx`: answers at a glance, trap-family targets, answers-and-traps table, trap legend, and a full explanation of every option.
- `rc_800_sectional_4.html`: the online timed test (auto-submits at 45:00; the key and explanations are encoded and decoded only after submitting).
- `source/`: `passages.py` and `questions.py` hold all content; `audit.py` checks option-length rank, absolutes, hedges, echo and the key pattern; `build_html.py`, `export.py` and `build_docx.js` build the three files.
